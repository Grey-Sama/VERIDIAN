# Veridian video user guide script

Use demonstration accounts and keep passwords hidden in the recording.

## Introduction

Welcome to Veridian, an Employee Leave Request System. This guide shows how employees request and track leave, and how administrators review requests and manage employee accounts.

## Employee walkthrough

1. Register and sign in. To create an account, select Create Employee Account and complete the required information. After registering, sign in using your username and password. The eye icon lets you show or hide your password.
2. Overview. The Overview page shows your available leave credits, request counts, and recent activity. New employees receive limited credits based on their employment category.
3. Request leave. Select Request Leave, choose a leave type, enter the number of weekdays, and select a start date. Veridian calculates the end date. Enter your reason, review the details, and submit. Fast Leave covers one half-day and is limited to three uses per year.
4. Track requests. Open My Requests to check decisions and remarks. You can cancel a pending request. Approved Leaves shows confirmed leave separately, while Leave Calendar displays your approved dates.
5. Balances and settings. Leave Balances shows your allowance, approved usage, pending requests, and available credits. In Account Settings, you can change your username, password, work details, and profile picture. Select Sign Out when finished.

## Administrator walkthrough

1. Sign in and review. Administrators use the same sign-in page. Their workspace shows employee requests and organization-wide activity.
2. Approve or reject leave. Open Leave Approvals and select View Details on a pending request. Review the dates, reason, and available credits. Add remarks if needed, then select Approve or Reject.
3. Check the calendar and balances. Leave Calendar shows approved employee leave and who is away today. In Leave Balances, search for an employee and click their name. The popup shows their credits and lets you change their employment category to Full-Time, Part-Time, or New Employee. Select Save Category to apply the change.
4. Manage accounts. The Employees page contains the employee directory. Under Create Accounts, administrators can create employee accounts. AdminA can also create other administrators.
5. AdminA controls. AdminA can reset an employee password. A popup displays a temporary password, which must be shared privately with the employee. The employee must change it after signing in. AdminA can also delete accounts after confirming the exact username. Demonstrate deletion only with a disposable account.

## Closing

That completes the Veridian user guide. Employees can request and track their leave, while administrators manage approvals, employment categories, and accounts. Always sign out when finished.
