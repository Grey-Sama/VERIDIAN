✳   A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straightforward answers.

What is Veridian?

Veridian is an Employee Leave Request System for managing time off, approvals, balances, and employee records.

Who is the system for?

Employees request and track leave. Administrators review requests and manage employee allowances.

What types of leave can I request?

Annual, sick, emergency, personal, and Fast Leave.

How do approvals work?

An administrator approves or rejects pending requests and may add remarks.

Can I view previous requests?

Yes. Your history includes requests, decisions, and remarks.

How are leave days counted?

Monday–Friday only. Holidays count. Pending requests reserve available days.

How many leave days are available?

The yearly allowance is 15 Annual, 15 Sick, and 7 Emergency days. Personal leave shares Annual credits. Fast Leave allows 3 uses per year. Your administrator can adjust full-day allowances.

Can I see leave on a calendar?

Yes. Admins see all approved employee leaves. Employees see their own approved leaves.

Who creates accounts?

Administrators create employee accounts. Admin A can also create administrator accounts.
