✳   A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straightforward answers.

What is Veridian?

Veridian is an Employee Leave Request System for managing time off, approvals, balances, and employee records.

Who is the system for?

Employees request and track leave. Administrators review requests and manage employee allowances.

What types of leave can I request?

Annual, sick, emergency, personal, and Fast Leave.

How do approvals work?

An administrator approves or rejects pending requests and may add remarks.

Can I view previous requests?

Yes. Your history includes requests, decisions, and remarks.

How are leave days counted?

Monday–Friday only. Holidays count. Pending requests reserve available days.

How many leave days are available?

Full credits are 15 Annual, 15 Sick, and 7 Emergency days. Personal leave shares Annual credits. Fast Leave allows 3 half-day uses per year. Full-Time and Part-Time receive full credits. New Employees start with 3 Sick days; Annual, Personal, and Emergency leave unlock when changed to Full-Time. Fast Leave remains 3 uses.

Can I see leave on a calendar?

Yes. Admins see all approved employee leaves. Employees see their own approved leaves.

Who creates accounts?

Employees can register from the sign-in page. Administrators can create employees. Only Admin A can create administrators.

Do administrators use a different sign-in?

No. Everyone uses the same sign-in page. Account permissions determine the workspace.

What information is needed to register?

Name, username, email, job role, department or office, and a password.

Does my job title give administrator access?

No. Job details describe your work. Administrator access is granted only through an account created by Admin A.
